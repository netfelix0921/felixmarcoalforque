How I work

Technology Built Around Real Business Problems.

I'm an ERP System Analyst at Eagle Cement Corporation and a hands-on web and app developer based in Manila. Most of my week is spent close to the business — analysing tickets and issues, troubleshooting the system, running tests and UAT, and working through processes with the people who actually use them.

Before that I was at Chase Technologies Corporation, leading and supporting POS and ERP implementations for clients across a range of industries. That combination is the whole point: I build web applications the way I'd design a business system — around the workflow first.
